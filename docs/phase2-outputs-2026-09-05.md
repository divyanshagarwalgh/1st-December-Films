## The read


A thirty second domestic slice built on one silent gesture: an accidental ketchup heart that the mother chooses not to wipe away, and a smudged heart drawn back inside the lid at the end. The dialogue is decoration; the film lives or dies on whether the two ketchup marks read instantly on a small screen and whether the boy's hidden smile in the classroom is real rather than performed. Everything else, rain, uniforms, the wall of umbrellas, is texture around those three frames.

## Beat sheet

- Radha packs two tiffins in a cramped kitchen before seven, rain on the window.
- Arjun asks about Ronak's mother's pasta; Radha answers without looking up.
- She squeezes ketchup into the compartment and it lands as a heart by accident.
- She notices, wipes the nozzle, leaves the heart.
- Arjun runs through umbrellas into school without turning back.
- Forty tiffin lids open at once in the classroom.
- Arjun sees the heart; two boys tease him.
- He dips a paratha corner, eats, hides a smile.
- Evening: Radha washes the tiffin and finds a heart drawn back inside the lid.
- Super, pack shot, voice-over.

## Runtime and format


Two spoken lines, one tease line and a closing voice-over, across ten beats. That is a lot of beats for thirty seconds, so the school gate and the classroom wide will each be one shot, not a scene. As written it plays closer to thirty five to forty in a first cut; getting to thirty means losing either the gate or the teasing boys. TVC as the primary, with the fifteen second television cut built as kitchen, heart, classroom, lid reveal, and the six second bumper as the ketchup squeeze into the heart alone. That bumper shot needs its own dedicated tabletop time on the day and should not be scavenged from master coverage.

## Production breakdown


Locations: three, plus a second day part. A real flat kitchen shot for both a pre-dawn and an evening look, a school gate exterior, a classroom interior. Cast: two principals, a class of roughly forty children plus two featured boys, a teacher, background parents and umbrellas at the gate. Day throughout, with an early morning and a dusk look inside the same kitchen. Flags: kids, crowd, food, water for rain, product hero for the pack shot.

The ketchup heart is a food styling job, not a lucky accident. It needs a product specialist, multiple bottles, temperature and viscosity control, and probably a rig for the squeeze so the shape repeats take after take. The second heart, smudged but legible after washing, is a separate styling problem and may need a light clean-up pass in post.

Complexity: complex. Forty children on a school day, a rain unit and a hero food shot that has to work three separate ways all sit in the same schedule.
