TITLE: THE TIFFIN
Brand: Kissan (ketchup)
Duration: 30 seconds

OPEN ON a cramped Mumbai kitchen at 6.40 am. Rain against the window. RADHA, 38, is packing two steel tiffins at once. Her son ARJUN, 9, sits on the counter in his school uniform, socks in hand.

ARJUN: Ma, Ronak's mother makes pasta for tiffin.

RADHA (not looking up): Ronak's mother has a maid.

She slaps two parathas into the tiffin, reaches for the Kissan bottle, squeezes a fat spiral of ketchup into the small compartment. It lands in a perfect heart, by accident. She notices. She wipes the nozzle and does not fix it.

CUT TO: EXT. SCHOOL GATE - DAY. A wall of umbrellas. Arjun runs in without looking back.

CUT TO: INT. CLASSROOM - LUNCH. Forty tiffins open at once. Sound of forty steel lids. Arjun opens his. The heart. Two boys lean over.

BOY 1: Your mom is filmy.

Arjun shrugs, dips a paratha corner, eats. A small smile he hides.

CUT TO: INT. KITCHEN - EVENING. Radha washes the tiffin. Inside the lid, in ketchup, smudged but legible: a heart drawn back.

SFX: rain easing.

SUPER: Thoda pyaar, roz.
PACK SHOT: Kissan Fresh Tomato Ketchup, the family bottle.
VO (warm, female): Kissan. Har tiffin mein thoda pyaar.

END CARD.

Notes for production: real kitchen, not a set. Actual rain if the shoot lands in monsoon, otherwise rain machine. The classroom needs forty kids and a lunch bell. Two cutdowns: 15 seconds for TV, a 6 second bumper of the heart for digital.
