TITLE: THE LAST BALL AT 35,000 FEET
Brand: an international airline, India market
Duration: 60 seconds, digital first, with a 20 second TV cut

INT. AIRCRAFT CABIN - NIGHT FLIGHT. Economy, full. Most passengers asleep. RAVI, 40s, in 32C, is watching a cricket match on the seat-back screen with the intensity of a man whose team is one wicket from losing.

His neighbour, an elderly PARSI GENTLEMAN, 70s, wakes up, looks at the screen, looks at Ravi.

PARSI GENTLEMAN: Last over?

RAVI (not looking away): Six needed. One ball.

The gentleman sits up. Across the aisle a TEENAGER pulls out one earbud. A FLIGHT ATTENDANT pauses with the water trolley. One by one the cabin comes alive around the small screen, people leaning across aisles, a baby handed over a seat so a mother can see.

The bowler runs in. The whole cabin holds its breath. Ravi's hand is on the tray table. The batsman connects.

Silence. Then the cabin ERUPTS. Strangers hug. The Parsi gentleman is on his feet punching the air. The flight attendant is cheering with the trolley still in hand.

FLIGHT ATTENDANT (composing herself, into the PA): Ladies and gentlemen, we are... very happy to announce... a six.

Laughter through the cabin. Ravi slumps back, spent, smiling.

SUPER: Some flights you never forget.
VO: Fly with the ones who feel it with you.
END CARD: airline logo, route destinations.

Notes: needs a real or near-real aircraft cabin set with at least 120 extras. The match footage on the screen will need rights or a staged match. We would love a cricketer cameo in the final seat reveal if budget allows.
