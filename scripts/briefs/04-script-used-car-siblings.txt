TITLE: THE PROMISE
Brand: an online used car marketplace
Duration: 40 seconds, digital, with 20 and 10 second cutdowns

INT. LIVING ROOM - DAY. A middle class Pune flat. NEHA, 30, and her younger brother KUNAL, 24, on the sofa. Between them on the coffee table, a phone showing a car listing.

KUNAL: Di, you promised.

NEHA: I promised to help you buy a car. Not to buy you a car.

KUNAL: Same thing.

NEHA: It is not the same thing.

Their MOTHER passes behind them with a laundry basket.

MOTHER (without stopping): She is right.

KUNAL (to mother): You always take her side.

MOTHER (off, from the kitchen): She has a job.

Neha smirks. Kunal grabs the phone, scrolls, stops.

KUNAL: Okay. This one. Inspected. Fixed price. Seven day return.

NEHA (reading): Seven day return?

KUNAL: Seven days. If you do not like it, they take it back.

Beat. Neha looks at him.

NEHA: So I can return you too?

MOTHER (off): No returns on that one. I asked.

Kunal throws a cushion toward the kitchen. Neha laughs, takes the phone, taps buy.

SUPER: Inspected. Fixed price. Seven day returns.
VO: The used car you can trust. Even more than your brother.
END CARD.

Notes: one location, three actors, sync sound. Hindi dialogue with English supers. We want this to feel like a real family, overlapping lines, not a sitcom.
