TITLE: THE SPLIT
Brand: a payments app, new bill-splitting feature
Duration: 45 seconds, digital, vertical and horizontal versions

OPEN ON a restaurant table after dinner. Six friends, the bill has arrived. Everyone goes quiet. This is a documentary-style hidden camera feel, handheld.

RIYA (to the group): Okay, so who had the paneer?

Everyone starts talking at once. We push into the chaos. Then FREEZE FRAME. The frame turns into a flat 2D illustration of the same table, the friends become simple line drawings.

VO (dry, quick): This is the part of the evening where friendships end.

The illustration animates. Amounts float above each head, wrong, crossed out, recalculated. A tiny cartoon calculator explodes.

VO: Or.

The illustration snaps back to live action. Riya taps her phone. We see the app UI, real, crisp: the bill photographed, items assigned by dragging names, a total per person.

RIYA: Done. Paying now.

Five phones buzz in sequence. Close-ups: each friend sees their exact share. One of them, ARJUN, frowns.

ARJUN: I did not have the paneer.

RIYA (tapping): Fixed.

His phone buzzes again. Lower amount. He nods, satisfied.

VO: Split the bill. Keep the friends.

END CARD: app logo, download call to action. A 6 second cutdown of the freeze frame to illustration transition for pre-roll.

Notes: mix of live action and 2D animation. UI must be pixel accurate to the actual app, legal will check every screen. Six cast members, one restaurant location, night interior.
