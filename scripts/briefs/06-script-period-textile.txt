TITLE: THE TAILOR OF KALBADEVI
Brand: a heritage textile and suiting brand, 100th anniversary
Duration: 60 seconds, cinema and digital

EXT. KALBADEVI, BOMBAY - 1962 - DAY. Trams. Handcarts. A period street dressed end to end. Signage in Marathi and Gujarati. We track behind a young man, DEVEN, 20, carrying a bolt of cloth on his shoulder through the crowd.

INT. TAILOR'S SHOP - CONTINUOUS. A cramped shop, ceiling fans, a wall of fabric rolls. The MASTER TAILOR, 60s, does not look up.

MASTER: Late.

DEVEN: The mill was late.

MASTER: Then you should have been early.

Deven lays the bolt on the table. The master runs the cloth between his fingers for a long moment. Something in his face changes.

MASTER: This is the new weave?

DEVEN: First bolt out of the mill.

The master picks up his chalk and, without measuring, marks the cloth. Cut to a MONTAGE across the decades, the same shop, the same table: 1975, a wedding suit, a young couple. 1989, a boy's first blazer. 2004, a woman being fitted for her first office suit, the master is now Deven, older. 2024, Deven's granddaughter marks the cloth. Same chalk. Same confidence. She does not measure either.

VO (female, present day): Some things you do not need to measure. You just know.

SUPER: 100 years of knowing.
END CARD: brand logo, a bolt of cloth.

Notes: five period looks for the same shop interior. The 1962 street exterior is the expensive shot: at least one dressed block, period vehicles, 80 to 100 extras in costume. We are open to a partial set build with VFX extension for the street.
