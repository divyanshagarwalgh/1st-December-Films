TITLE: NO ROAD
Brand: an SUV launch, off-road capable variant
Duration: 45 seconds TVC, 60 second digital, plus 15 second cutdowns

EXT. HIMALAYAN ROAD - PRE-DAWN. Blue hour. A single SUV on a switchback, headlights cutting through mist. Drone wide, then a low tracking shot at wheel height as the tyres bite gravel.

VO (male, low): The map said the road ends here.

The SUV leaves the tarmac. Camera car alongside as it crosses a shallow river, water sheeting off the bonnet. Interior: the DRIVER, 35, a woman, unbothered, one hand on the wheel.

VO: It did not say anything about you.

A rockfall section. The SUV climbs it at an angle that makes the audience wince, shot from below with a long lens. Suspension articulates. A close-up of a wheel lifting clear of the ground and settling.

CUT TO a night section: the SUV on a ridge, stars, a slow orbit by drone with the headlights as the only light source.

EXT. RIDGE - DAWN. The driver steps out. Below her, a valley nobody has driven into. She looks at the phone. No signal. She smiles and puts it away.

SUPER: Made for where the road gives up.
END CARD: SUV name, variant, logo.

Notes: real locations in Himachal or Ladakh, 4 to 5 shoot days, precision driver, camera car, drone permits, water crossing safety, night shoot on a ridge. We need this to feel dangerous and be safe. Legal disclaimers on the stunt shots.
