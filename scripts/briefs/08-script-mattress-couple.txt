TITLE: THE TRIAL
Brand: a direct to consumer mattress brand, 100 night trial
Duration: 30 seconds, digital and TV

INT. BEDROOM - NIGHT. A couple in bed. SAMEER and DIVYA, both mid 30s. Lights off. Long silence.

DIVYA: Are you awake?

SAMEER: No.

DIVYA: It is too soft.

SAMEER: It is fine.

DIVYA: You said the last one was too hard.

SAMEER: The last one was too hard.

DIVYA: So this one is fine.

SAMEER: This one is fine.

Silence.

DIVYA: Night 43.

SAMEER (sitting up): You are counting?

DIVYA: They give you 100 nights. I am using them.

SAMEER: We are not returning a mattress we have slept on for 43 nights.

DIVYA: That is literally what the 100 nights are for.

Sameer lies back down. Silence.

SAMEER: It is a bit soft.

DIVYA (already reaching for her phone): I know.

SUPER: 100 night trial. Actually use it.
VO: The mattress you can send back. Even on night 43.
END CARD.

Notes: one bedroom set, two actors, whole thing plays in near darkness with a practical lamp turned on at the end. Sync sound. We want a deadpan, slightly British-comedy timing, not broad.
