TITLE: GHAR AA
Brand: a national jewellery chain, Diwali campaign
Duration: 90 second anthem film, 30 second TV cut, 20 second cutdowns per region

An original song carries the film. No dialogue. The lyric is about coming home, in Hindi with a Tamil and a Bengali version for the regional cuts.

OPEN ON a railway platform at dusk, festival crowd, a young woman, MEERA, stepping off a train with a small box in her hand.

MONTAGE, cut to the song:
A father in Jaipur polishing the doorway lamp.
A grandmother in Madurai threading jasmine, a gold bangle catching the light.
Kolkata, a rooftop, boys with sparklers, a mother watching from the doorway.
Meera in a taxi, the city lit up around her, the box on her lap.
A wedding hall being decorated, a bride's hands, a necklace being fastened.
A shop floor of the jewellery brand, families choosing, a small girl trying on earrings too big for her.
Meera at her family's door. Her mother opens it. Neither says anything. Meera opens the box: a pair of earrings, the same design her mother is wearing.

The chorus lands as the mother touches her own earring, understands, and pulls Meera in.

Final wide: the whole street lit with diyas, families on every balcony.

SUPER: Ghar aa. Diwali is where you are loved.
END CARD.

Notes: four cities minimum, crowd scenes with 150 plus extras for the street finale, a wedding set piece, original music to be composed and recorded before the shoot. Talent: we are considering a well known actress as Meera. Regional versions need to be shot, not dubbed, for the family scenes.
