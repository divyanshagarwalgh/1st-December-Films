TITLE: CRUNCH AND THE MONDAY MONSTER
Brand: a kids breakfast cereal
Duration: 30 seconds TV, 15 second cutdown, six 10 second social bumpers

INT. KITCHEN - MORNING. Live action. AARAV, 7, slumps at the breakfast table. It is Monday and he is not having it. His BOWL of cereal sits untouched.

From under the table, a fully animated 2D character, CRUNCH, a small orange lion with a spoon for a tail, climbs up and sits on the edge of the bowl. He is animated over the live plate, interacting with real milk splashes.

CRUNCH: Monday monster got you?

AARAV (deadpan): There is no Monday monster.

CRUNCH: Then why is your face like that?

Aarav almost smiles. Crunch dives into the bowl. A real milk splash hits Aarav's face. Aarav laughs. Crunch surfaces with a piece of cereal and flicks it at him. Aarav catches it in his mouth.

Cut to the MOTHER at the counter, back turned, hearing laughter.

MOTHER: What is funny?

AARAV (mouth full): Nothing.

Crunch winks at camera from behind the bowl, puts a finger to his lips.

SUPER: Beat the Monday monster.
PACK SHOT: the cereal box, Crunch on the front.
VO: New crunchier flakes. Even Monday cannot stop it.
END CARD.

Notes: the animated character has an existing model sheet from the brand. Needs a 2D animation studio and a compositor who can match milk physics. One child actor, one kitchen. The six social bumpers are Crunch alone, no live action, which could be done entirely by the animation studio.
