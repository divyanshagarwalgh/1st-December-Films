TITLE: THE EMPTY CHAIR
Brand: a road safety foundation, in partnership with a helmet manufacturer
Duration: 3 minute film for digital and cinema, 60 second cut

This is a documentary-led film with a scripted spine. We film real families who lost a two-wheeler rider, in their homes, and the scripted device is a single empty chair at every dining table.

OPEN ON a dining table in a small flat in Nashik. Five plates. Four people. A woman in her 50s, SUNITA, serves food and sets a plate at the empty place out of habit, then stops.

SUNITA (to camera, in Marathi, subtitled): He always sat there. I still put the plate. I do not know how to stop.

CUT TO a second family in Coimbatore. A father, a teenage daughter, an empty chair between them. The daughter talks about the last phone call.

CUT TO a third family in Lucknow. A young widow, 28. Her son, 4, sits in the empty chair and she moves him gently to another one.

Intercut with these, a scripted sequence: a rider at dawn on an empty highway, helmet on the handlebar, not on his head. We never see a crash. We see the helmet, still on the handlebar, at the side of the road, and the bike on its side. Then the chair.

VO (male, quiet): A helmet takes four seconds to wear. The chair stays empty for the rest of their lives.

Final sequence: all three families at their tables, the empty chair, then a hard cut to a young rider fastening a helmet strap. He rides off. Cut to his family's table. All chairs full.

SUPER: Four seconds.
END CARD: foundation logo, helmet brand logo, a helpline.

Notes: real families, consent and care needed. Three cities. Small crew, natural light, handheld. The highway sequence is the only staged material and needs a stunt coordinator even though we do not show the crash.
