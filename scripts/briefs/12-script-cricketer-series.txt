TITLE: THE ROUTINE (4 film series)
Brand: an energy drink, cricket sponsorship
Format: four 20 second films, one per week during the tournament, plus 6 second bumpers

Each film follows the same structure: a real international cricketer (contracted brand ambassador, name to be confirmed, one of the top five Indian batters) doing one small pre-match routine, interrupted by the product.

FILM 1: THE GLOVE
INT. DRESSING ROOM. The cricketer laces his gloves in a precise order. Right, left, right again. A TEAMMATE (actor) watches.
TEAMMATE: Every time?
CRICKETER: Every time.
He reaches for the drink. Opens it. Takes a sip. Puts it down in the exact same spot.
TEAMMATE: And that?
CRICKETER: Especially that.
SUPER: Part of the routine.

FILM 2: THE STEP
EXT. BOUNDARY ROPE. The cricketer steps over the rope with his right foot first, always. He goes back and does it again because a kid distracted him. Sip from the drink. Then the step.

FILM 3: THE TAP
INT. NETS. Two taps of the bat on the crease. A bowler waits. The cricketer holds up a hand, walks back, has a sip, returns. Two taps.

FILM 4: THE NOD
INT. TUNNEL. Before walking out, the cricketer nods once at nothing. Teammate: Who are you nodding at? Cricketer, holding the can: Him. He taps the can. Walks out.

END CARD on all four: product, tournament logo, brand logo.

Notes: talent availability is one day, all four films plus bumpers must be shot in that day, at a stadium or a convincing set. Talent has approval over the scripts. Legal: no on-field footage from the tournament can be used.
