CREATIVE BRIEF

Brand: a mass premium shower gel range, new fragrance line (five variants)
Agency: (in house brand team)

Objective: launch the new range to women 22 to 35 in metro and tier one cities. Establish the idea that this shower gel changes your mood, not just your skin. Drive trial in the first eight weeks.

Target audience: working women, urban, sharing a bathroom with family or flatmates, for whom the shower is the only ten minutes of the day that belong to them. They buy on fragrance first and price second. They see through anything that looks like a "spa" cliche.

Key message: five fragrances, five moods. Pick the one your day needs.

Proposition: a shower that resets the day.

Tone of voice: warm, playful, a little knowing. Not aspirational, not serene. Real bathrooms, real mornings, a sense of humour about the chaos outside the door.

Deliverables: one 30 second launch TVC, five 15 second variant films (one per fragrance, same structure), vertical edits for Instagram and YouTube Shorts, three 6 second bumpers. Hindi master, Tamil and Telugu adaptations.

Mandatories: all five packs must appear in the launch film. New logo lockup. The end line "Pick your mood" is fixed. No competitor references.

Platforms: TV (GECs and movie channels), YouTube, Instagram, Hotstar.

Timeline: on air in ten weeks. Shoot within five weeks.

Budget: mid range for the category. We are open to a single day shoot if the idea allows.

KPIs: awareness lift among the target, trial in the first eight weeks, view-through rate on the 15 second films.

What we need from a production partner: a director who can get performance out of non-actors, since we want real women where possible, and a treatment that makes five variants feel like one campaign without shooting five films.
