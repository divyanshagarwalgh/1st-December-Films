BRIEF: PERFORMANCE FILMS, QUICK COMMERCE

Brand: a 10 minute grocery and essentials delivery app
Objective: drive first orders in three new cities (Indore, Lucknow, Coimbatore) through paid social. These are performance assets, not brand films. Success is cost per first order.

Target audience: 25 to 40, households where both partners work, currently ordering from a competitor or from the local kirana on the phone.

Key message: whatever you forgot, it is at your door in ten minutes.

Deliverables: twelve 10 to 15 second vertical films for Instagram and YouTube Shorts, in three languages (Hindi, Tamil, English), built as a modular set so hooks and end cards can be swapped. Plus static frames pulled from the films.

Tone: quick, funny, unpolished on purpose. We want these to look like they were shot on a phone by a real person, not like an ad. The joke is always the small domestic emergency: the milk that ran out at 6 am, the guest who arrived early, the exam morning with no pen.

Mandatories: the app UI in every film, the delivery rider in the brand jacket at the door, the ten minute timer on screen.

Platforms: Meta and YouTube paid only. No TV.

Timeline: three weeks from brief to live. We will run the first six films, read the data, and brief the next six on what worked.

Budget: low per film, this is volume. We would rather have twelve rough films than four beautiful ones.

Questions we have: can a production house do this at this pace and price, or is this a content studio job? Should we shoot all twelve in one day in one house?
