BRIEF: TEACHERS' DAY FILM

Brand: an online learning platform for school students (classes 6 to 12)
Objective: a Teachers' Day film that earns goodwill with parents and teachers, both of whom are sceptical of edtech. This is not a sales film. No offer, no pricing, no app demo.

Target audience: primarily parents of school-going children in tier one and two cities, secondarily teachers themselves, who we want to share the film.

Insight: every adult remembers one teacher who saw something in them before they saw it in themselves. Most never told that teacher.

Key message: the platform exists to support teachers, not replace them.

Tone of voice: sincere, restrained, emotional without manipulation. We have been burned before by a film that felt manufactured and got called out online. Real stories preferred over scripted ones.

Deliverables: one hero film, 2 to 3 minutes, for YouTube and Instagram. One 60 second cut. A 30 second cut for pre-roll. Subtitled in six languages.

Mandatories: the brand appears only at the end, as a supporter of the film. Teachers featured must be real, currently teaching, and paid for their time.

Platforms: YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, PR seeding to education press.

Timeline: Teachers' Day is in seven weeks. Film must be live two days before.

Budget: modest. We would rather spend on finding the right people than on production scale.

Open questions for the production house: should this be documentary, or scripted with real teachers playing themselves? How do we find the stories? What is the risk of it feeling like every other Teachers' Day film?
