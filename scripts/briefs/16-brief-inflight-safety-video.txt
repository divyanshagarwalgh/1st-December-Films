BRIEF: IN-FLIGHT SAFETY VIDEO

Brand: a full service Indian airline
Objective: replace the current safety demonstration video with one passengers actually watch. Regulatory content is fixed and must be shown in full and in order. The creative wraps around it.

Target audience: every passenger, every flight. Frequent flyers who have stopped watching, first time flyers who are anxious, families with children, international passengers who do not speak Hindi.

Key message: we take safety seriously and we take you seriously.

Idea territory we like: the demonstration performed across India, one instruction per location, by real crew, with a light musical thread. Seat belt on a houseboat in Kerala, oxygen mask in a Ladakh monastery, brace position in a Mumbai local, and so on. Other territories welcome.

Deliverables: one 4 to 5 minute film in English with Hindi subtitles, a Hindi language version, a shorter international version. Exact runtime is determined by the regulatory script.

Mandatories: every regulatory instruction verbatim, demonstrated clearly by uniformed crew, with graphics where the regulator requires. Approval from the airline's safety department and the regulator before release. No humour that undermines an instruction.

Platforms: seat-back screens, overhead cabin screens, YouTube.

Timeline: five months, including two approval rounds.

Budget: this is a one-time asset that plays millions of times; the airline is prepared to invest in it properly.

Questions for the production house: how many locations is realistic? How do we keep the instructions legible on a small overhead screen? Have you handled regulated content before?
