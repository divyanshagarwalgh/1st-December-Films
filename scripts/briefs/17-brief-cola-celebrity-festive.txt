BRIEF: FESTIVE FILM WITH BRAND AMBASSADOR

Brand: a cola brand with a strong masculine, action-led identity
Objective: own the festive season (Diwali and the regional festivals around it) with a film starring the brand's ambassador, a top South Indian film star. Drive consumption occasions at family gatherings and festive parties.

Target audience: 18 to 30, male skew, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, with a Hindi version for the north.

Key message: the festival is more fun with a little more attitude.

Proposition: the bold festive drink.

Tone: big, energetic, a wink of humour, action set pieces are welcome but must be family-safe for festive viewing. The star's persona is the engine; the script should give him a signature moment.

Deliverables: one 60 second film, 30 and 20 second cuts, Telugu master with Tamil, Kannada and Hindi versions (dubbed, star records all four), plus social cutdowns and a 15 second pre-roll.

Mandatories: the bottle in the hero shot, the brand's signature end sting, the star's contractual screen time minimum (agency will share), festive decor visible.

Platforms: TV, cinema, YouTube, Instagram, Hotstar during the cricket.

Timeline: star availability is two days in early September. Air by mid October.

Budget: the star fee is separate. Production budget is healthy, but the two-day window is fixed and we need a partner who can plan a big film around it.

Questions: what can realistically be shot with the star in two days? What can be shot without him? How do we make four language versions feel native and not dubbed?
