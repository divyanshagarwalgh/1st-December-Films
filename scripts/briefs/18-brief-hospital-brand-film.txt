BRIEF: HOSPITAL CHAIN BRAND FILM

Brand: a multi-city private hospital chain, 25th anniversary
Objective: a brand film that repositions the chain from "the expensive hospital" to "the hospital that tells you the truth". Internal audiences (12,000 staff) are as important as the public.

Target audience: families in the decision-making moment, 35 to 60, who choose a hospital for a parent or a child. Secondary: doctors and nurses the chain wants to recruit and retain.

Insight: patients do not remember the equipment. They remember whether someone looked them in the eye and explained what was happening.

Key message: we tell you what is going on, in words you understand, every time.

Tone: honest, warm, unhurried. No stock footage of corridors and scanners. No actors playing patients if we can avoid it. We would accept a hybrid of real staff and scripted patient moments.

Deliverables: one 2 minute brand film, a 60 second cut, five 30 second stories (one per city) for regional use, internal version with a message from the chairman.

Mandatories: doctors and nurses shown must be real employees. Patient consent process to be managed with the hospital's legal team. Brand identity refresh (new logo) to be introduced at the end.

Platforms: YouTube, cinema in five cities, internal screens, LinkedIn.

Timeline: anniversary event in four months. Film premieres there.

Budget: substantial, this is a once in a decade film for them.

Questions for the production house: how do you film in a working hospital without disrupting care? How do you get real doctors to be natural on camera? Have you done multi-city films with a common spine?
