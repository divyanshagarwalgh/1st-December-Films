Salary as per industry standards with an annual bonus. Five day week, hybrid after probation. Immediate joiners preferred.

Apply with a CV and a short note on the most complicated reconciliation you have handled. Contact HR through the careers page. Shortlisted candidates will be called for two rounds, one technical and one with the finance head.
